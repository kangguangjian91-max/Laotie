## Why Steel for Sports Facilities?

### Unmatched Clear Span Capability

Sports facilities need column-free interiors. Steel trusses and space frames achieve spans of **40 to 200+ meters** without intermediate columns — perfect for basketball courts, swimming pools, and indoor tracks.

### Construction Speed

A steel sports hall can be erected in **6–12 weeks** after foundation work, compared to 4–8 months for reinforced concrete. This is critical when projects must align with academic calendars or tournament seasons.

### Flexibility & Future Expansion

Steel buildings are inherently **modular and extendable**. Need to add a wing or expand seating capacity? Steel makes retrofitting straightforward and cost-effective.

### Durability in All Climates

With proper **hot-dip galvanizing and weather-resistant coatings**, steel sports facilities last 50+ years with minimal maintenance — even in coastal or high-humidity environments.

## Structural Systems for Sports Venues

### Portal Frame System (25–50 m span)

The simplest and most economical option for smaller gymnasiums:

- **Roof pitch**: 5–15° (single slope or double slope)
- **Frame spacing**: 6–8 m typical
- **Members**: Welded H-section columns and rafters
- **Bracing**: Cross bracing in roof and wall planes
- **Cost advantage**: Lowest cost per square meter

### Space Frame System (40–200 m span)

The go-to solution for large sports halls and arenas:

- **Module size**: 2.5–4.5 m (smaller for heavy loads)
- **Depth**: L/15 to L/20 of span
- **Node connection**: Bolted sphere (MERO system) or welded
- **Roof covering**: Standing seam metal roof, membrane, or glass
- **Key advantage**: Multiple support points possible — ideal for irregular plans

### Truss System (30–100 m span)

Pipe trusses or lattice trusses for medium-to-large venues:

- **Configuration**: Triangular or rectangular cross-section
- **Typical spacing**: 6–12 m between trusses
- **Purlins**: C/Z purlins at 1.2–1.5 m spacing
- **Material**: Circular hollow section (CHS) for chords, angles for web members

## Critical Design Parameters

### 1. Roof Live Load

Sports facilities have unique roof loading requirements:

| Load Type | Standard Value | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Roof live load | 0.5–0.75 kN/m² | Per ASCE 7 / Eurocode |
| Suspended equipment | 0.3–1.0 kN/m² | Scoreboard, lighting, HVAC |
| Acoustic panels | 0.15–0.30 kN/m² | Absorption panels + grid |
| Catwalk / maintenance | 1.0–2.0 kN/m² | Walkways for lighting access |

### 2. Deflection Limits

Stricter than industrial buildings due to ceiling and equipment requirements:

| Member | Deflection Limit | Purpose |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| Roof beam/truss | L/250 (live load) | Prevent ceiling cracking |
| Roof purlin | L/180 | Cladding integrity |
| Cantilever canopy | L/180 | Visual + drainage |
| Floor beam | L/360 | Vibration control |

### 3. Vibration Control

Sports facilities face unique dynamic loads:

- **Rhythmic crowd movement**: Design natural frequency > 5 Hz
- **Impact loads**: Basketball backboards, gymnastics equipment
- **Wind-induced vibration**: Lightweight steel roofs susceptible to uplift

**Solution**: Add tuned mass dampers for large-span roofs, increase structural depth to raise stiffness, and use composite floor decks for spectator areas.

## FAQ

### What is the minimum span for a steel sports hall?

A standard basketball court requires a 28 m × 15 m clear area plus 2 m sidelines, so the minimum structural span is typically **25 m**. For multi-court facilities, spans of 35–50 m are common.

### How long does a steel sports facility last?

With proper **hot-dip galvanizing (≥85 µm zinc coating)** and maintenance every 5 years (cleaning, touch-up painting), a steel sports structure can last **50–70 years** in most environments.

### Can a steel sports hall be air-conditioned?

Absolutely. Insulated sandwich panels (40–100 mm PU/PIR core) combined with HVAC systems achieve excellent thermal performance. The lightweight structure typically requires less cooling capacity than concrete buildings.

### Is fire protection required for steel sports venues?

Yes. Depending on local building codes, you may need **intumescent coating (60–120 minute rating)** or spray-applied fireproofing. We include fire protection design as part of our engineering package.
