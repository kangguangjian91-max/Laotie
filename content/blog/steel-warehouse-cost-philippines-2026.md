## 7 Hidden Costs Most Philippine Suppliers Won't Mention

### 1. Site Preparation & Soil Testing
If your land is sloping or has poor soil (common in Cavite, Laguna hillside lots), site leveling and soil improvement can add ₱300–₱600/sqm before a single column goes up.

### 2. PEZA / BOI Compliance Upgrades
If you're in a PEZA zone, you may need fire-rated sandwich panels (₱1,200–₱1,800/sqm wall instead of ₱600–₱800 for single skin), dedicated fire hydrant lines, and emergency exits — collectively adding 8–15% to project cost.

### 3. Crane Beam Reinforcement
Planning to install an overhead crane later? Adding crane beam capacity during initial design costs ~₱200–₱400/sqm extra. Retrofitting later costs 3× more.

### 4. Insulation (Thermal)
Philippine warehouses without insulation hit 40°C+ inside at noon. 50mm EPS sandwich panel roof adds ₱400–₱600/sqm but cuts cooling costs and protects stored goods.

### 5. Typhoon Wind Load Engineering
Your steel frame must be designed to NSCP 2015 wind zone requirements. Coastal sites (Subic, Batangas, Cebu) need heavier sections and additional bracing — adding ₱150–₱300/sqm over inland sites.

### 6. Customs Clearance & Broker Fees
Expect 5–8% of FOB value for PH customs brokerage, BOC assessment, and incidental port charges. A ₱2M steel shipment might cost ₱100K–₱160K to clear.

### 7. Mezzanine Floor
If your 500 sqm warehouse needs a 200 sqm office mezzanine, budget an additional ₱1,200–₱1,800/sqm of mezzanine area for steel decking, concrete topping, stairs, and railings.

## How to Get an Accurate Steel Warehouse Quote

The worst approach: email 3 suppliers saying "I need a warehouse, how much?" The best approach:

1. **Provide exact dimensions**: Length × Width × Eave Height (not just "500 sqm")
2. **Specify wind zone**: Tell them your city/municipality so they engineer to the correct NSCP wind load
3. **Clarify insulation**: Single skin or sandwich panel? If sandwich: EPS, PU, or Rockwool? What thickness?
4. **List doors and openings**: How many roll-up doors? What size? Any personnel doors?
5. **Mention crane plans**: Even if it's "maybe in 3 years" — design for it now
6. **Ask for BOQ (Bill of Quantities)**: Not just a total price. You need to see kg of steel, sqm of cladding, number of bolts. This is the only way to compare quotes.
