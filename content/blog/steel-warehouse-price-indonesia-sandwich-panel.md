# Steel Warehouse Price Indonesia 2026: Sandwich Panel vs Single Skin

Indonesian warehouse buyers face a critical decision early in their project: sandwich panel cladding or plain single skin steel sheets? The difference isn't just aesthetics — it changes your total warehouse price by 30–50% and determines whether your building is usable year-round in Indonesia's tropical climate.

This guide breaks down real steel warehouse prices in Indonesia for 2026, comparing the two cladding options across material costs, installation, and long-term value — with data from projects in Jakarta, Surabaya, and Batam.

## Single Skin Steel: The Budget Option

Single skin means one layer of 0.4–0.5mm steel sheet (zinc-coated or galvalume) for both roof and walls. It's the cheapest steel warehouse you can build in Indonesia.

### Pros
- **Lowest upfront cost**: Rp 1.2–1.8M/sqm fully installed
- **Fast construction**: 4–6 weeks for a 1,000 sqm warehouse
- **Simple**: No insulation to spec, no adhesives, fewer components

### Cons
- **Extreme heat inside**: Without insulation, interior temperatures can reach 45°C+ at midday in Surabaya or Jakarta
- **Condensation problems**: Humid tropical air hits the cool steel surface at night → water dripping onto stored goods
- **High noise during rain**: Heavy tropical rain on a single-skin roof sounds like a drum
- **Higher cooling cost**: If you add AC later, energy bills will be significant

### When Single Skin Makes Sense
- Agricultural equipment storage (tractors, harvesters)
- Temporary construction site warehouses (<2 years)
- Open-sided buildings where ventilation is the priority
- Projects with absolutely minimal budget

## Sandwich Panels: The Real-World Standard

Sandwich panels consist of two steel skins bonded to an insulating core. For Indonesian conditions, this is what 80% of our clients ultimately choose after understanding the trade-offs.

### EPS Core (Expanded Polystyrene)
- **Thickness**: 50mm or 75mm
- **Cost adder vs single skin**: +Rp 400K–500K/sqm
- **Thermal performance**: Reduces heat transfer by 60–70% vs single skin
- **Best for**: General dry storage warehouses, logistics centers

### PU/PIR Core (Polyurethane / Polyisocyanurate)
- **Thickness**: 50mm or 75mm
- **Cost adder vs single skin**: +Rp 900K–1,100K/sqm
- **Thermal performance**: Reduces heat transfer by 80–90%
- **Fire rating**: PIR achieves B1 (self-extinguishing)
- **Best for**: Cold storage, food processing, pharmaceutical warehouses

### Rockwool Core (Mineral Wool)
- **Thickness**: 50mm or 75mm
- **Cost adder vs single skin**: +Rp 1,200K–1,500K/sqm
- **Thermal performance**: Good, with superior fire resistance (A1 rating)
- **Acoustic performance**: Excellent sound absorption
- **Best for**: Fire-rated industrial facilities, buildings near residential areas

## FAQ

### What's the most common cladding choice for Indonesian warehouses?
EPS sandwich panel, 50mm thickness. It hits the sweet spot of thermal performance and cost. Most logistics warehouses and dry storage facilities in Jabodetabek and Surabaya use this specification.

### Does sandwich panel prevent rust in Indonesia's humid climate?
The panel itself resists rust when using AZ150 (galvalume) coated steel skins. However, the cut edges and joints need proper flashing and sealant — this is where poor installation causes problems. Always verify your installer's flashing details.

### How long does it take to ship panels from China to Indonesia?
Standard sea freight: 8–12 days. Customs clearance at Tanjung Priok: 3–5 days. Total: 2–3 weeks from factory to Jakarta site. Surabaya (Tanjung Perak) adds 2–3 days for inter-island shipping.

### Can I mix single skin walls with a sandwich panel roof?
Yes, and it's a common budget strategy. The roof gets the most solar radiation, so insulating it with sandwich panel is high ROI. Walls can be single skin where direct sun exposure is limited.

### What thickness do I need for cold storage?
Minimum 75mm PU/PIR. For frozen storage (–18°C and below), go to 100mm or 150mm. The additional thickness pays for itself in reduced compressor runtime.
