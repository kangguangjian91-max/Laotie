## 正文

**Shangqiu, Henan, China — July 8, 2026** — Henan Laotie Steel Structure Engineering Co., Ltd., a CE (EN 1090-1) and ISO 9001:2015 certified steel structure manufacturer, today announced expanded export operations serving 30+ countries across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Australia, and South America.

The company operates a 20,000-square-meter factory in Shangqiu, Henan Province — China's steel manufacturing hub — with 5 fully automated production lines and a monthly output capacity of 5,000 tons. Over 200 skilled engineers and workers support end-to-end steel structure solutions covering engineering design, fabrication, surface treatment, container loading, and shipping logistics.

"International buyers are increasingly looking for reliable Chinese steel structure manufacturers who can deliver both quality and competitive pricing," said Kang Guangjian, General Manager of Laotie Steel Structure. "With 15 years of manufacturing experience and certification to international standards, we provide a one-stop solution that takes the complexity out of importing steel buildings."

### Product Lines

Laotie Steel Structure specializes in:

- **Pre-engineered steel buildings** for warehouses, factories, and commercial use
- **Aircraft hangars** with large-span capabilities
- **Agricultural buildings** including poultry houses, barns, and greenhouses
- **Multi-story steel structures** for office and commercial complexes
- **Space frame and roof truss systems**
- **Floor deck, steel decking, and cladding systems**

### Certifications and Quality

All products comply with international standards including ASTM (A36, A572), EN (S235, S275, S355), and GB (Q235B, Q355B). Surface treatment options range from standard primer to hot-dip galvanizing and fireproof coating, with coating specifications meeting C1 through C5-M environmental classifications.

The company was founded in 2009 and is headquartered in Shangqiu, Henan Province, with an international trade coordination office in Shenzhen, China.
